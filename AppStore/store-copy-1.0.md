## Description（en-US）

**description · en-US — 3409 / 4000 字元**

```
Record part of your screen, talk over it, and let your Mac write the captions. Trim it, pick a shape, and the clip is ready to post.

The captions are produced on your own Mac. No account to create, no cloud service, nothing uploaded, no data collected.

Soon-Cut is built for the videos you record to explain something: software walkthroughs, tutorials, product demos, bug reports, commentary over your own work, the few minutes of a meeting worth keeping.

RECORD ONLY WHAT MATTERS
- Drag a box around the part of the screen you need, and only that part is recorded. One click locks the box to 9:16 for vertical video.
- Separate switches for system audio and microphone: the computer's sound, your voice, both, or silence.
- The panel floats above everything without taking focus, so whatever you are demonstrating keeps playing and keeps the keyboard.
- A faint dashed outline marks the region and turns red while you record.
- Record take after take; each one lands in the clip strip. Already have footage? Import MP4, MOV or M4V files, or drag them in.

CUT IT DOWN
- Trim the head and tail on a filmstrip.
- Remove something from the middle: park the playhead, press Command-B to split, then pull the two pieces apart. The gap is skipped in the preview and in the export.
- Drag thumbnails sideways to reorder them. Drop a clip into the staging area to leave it out of this video without deleting the file.

CAPTIONS WITHOUT A SERVER
- Speech to text runs on your Mac with WhisperKit. Ten spoken languages plus auto-detect: Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Thai, Vietnamese and Indonesian.
- Need the subtitles in a different language from the speech? Apple's on-device translation covers eleven subtitle languages, Traditional and Simplified Chinese included. macOS may ask you to download a language pack the first time.
- Every line stays editable. Click a timestamp to jump there, rewrite the line, or delete it.

ONE RECORDING, FIVE SHAPES
- Export at the original ratio, 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9. Where the footage does not fill the frame, a blurred copy of itself fills the background.
- Each ratio lists what it suits and flags a video that runs longer than those places allow.
- 1080p, 720p or 540p, each with an estimated file size before you start.
- Captions are burned into the picture, so they survive the upload.

AND THEN OUT
- Finished videos collect in one list with a preview. Drag a card straight into an upload box in your browser, or into Finder.
- Mark what you have posted, archive what you are done with.

FREE, WITH ONE OPTIONAL PURCHASE
Every feature above is free to use. Exported videos carry a small "SoonCut" mark in the lower-right corner, and a single in-app purchase removes it on every Mac signed in to your Apple Account. One payment, not a subscription, nothing to renew.

WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD
- Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac.
- The first time you generate captions, Soon-Cut downloads a speech model of about 1.6 GB and then takes a minute or two to prepare it for your Mac. That happens once; after that, captions take seconds. Never use captions and nothing is downloaded.
- The interface is in Traditional Chinese.
- Soon-Cut is open source, released under the GPL-3.0.

Record what is yours to record. Soon-Cut is made for your own screen, your own work, and commentary you have the right to publish.
```

【只錄需要的那一塊】
- 在畫面上拖一個框，就只錄那一塊。按一下 9:16 直接鎖成直式。

- 可以連續錄好幾段，每段都進片段列。已經有影片？按匯入，或把 MP4、MOV、M4V 直接拖進來。

【剪掉不要的】
- 拖膠卷條兩端的黃色把手剪頭尾。
- 想拿掉中間：把播放線停在要切的位置，⌘B 剪開，再把兩塊拉開，中間的空隙在預覽與輸出都會跳過。
- 左右拖縮圖排順序。往下拖進暫存區，這次就不輸出，檔案還留著。

【字幕不用連伺服器】

- 每一句都能改。點時間跳到那一句，直接重寫或刪掉。

【一次錄影，五種比例】
- 原始、9:16、4:5、1:1、16:9 都能輸出。畫面填不滿的地方，用自己的模糊版本補背景。
- 每個比例都會標出適合發在哪裡，影片超過那些地方的長度上限時會提醒你。
- 1080p、720p、540p 三種畫質，按下輸出前就先看到預估檔案大小。
- 字幕是燒進畫面的，上傳到哪裡都不會掉。

【輸出之後】
- 輸出的影片集中在一份清單裡，可以預覽。把卡片直接拖進瀏覽器的上傳框，或拖到 Finder。
- 發完標記「已上傳」，用不到的封存起來。

【免費，只有一個選購項目】
上面所有功能都免費。輸出的影片右下角會有一個小小的「SoonCut」，一次購買即可移除，同一個 Apple 帳號的其他 Mac 也一起生效。一次買斷，不是訂閱，不會自動續訂。

- Soon-Cut 以 GPL-3.0 開源。

錄你有權利錄的東西。Soon-Cut 是給你自己的畫面、自己的作品，以及你有權公開的講評用的。
